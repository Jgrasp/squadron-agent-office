# Changelog

## 0.2.0 · 2026-11-18

- The office opens on its own when Squadron starts; turn it off in Settings › Extensions › Agent Office
- The Agent Office button leaves the agent's tools: open the office again from its settings, which show whether it is open and what your agents are doing
- Names show only over agents that wait for you, failed or are unread, never while they walk, and stay sharp

## 0.1.0 · 2026-11-17

- First version: your agents at work in an isometric office, one room per project and one block per group, live, in a floating window of its own
- A status wall with Claude's session and weekly limits and the state of its API
- Click an agent to see what it does and open it; click an office to count its agents
- New layout, and the ★ menu to keep a layout and open with it
